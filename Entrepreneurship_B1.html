B1 Grammar: Relative Clauses with “who” and “which”

We use who for people and which for things.

UseExample
who = peopleAn entrepreneur is a person who starts a business.
which = thingsAn app is a product which people use on a phone.

  5. What business idea would help people in your city?
  6. What risks do entrepreneurs take?
  7. Should schools teach entrepreneurship?
