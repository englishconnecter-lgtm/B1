B1 Grammar: Past Simple for Biographies

Use the Past Simple to describe finished events in someone’s life. Biographies often use dates and time expressions.

FormUseExample
subject + was/werebirth, age, identity, locationEinstein was born in 1879.
subject + regular verb + -edfinished actionsHe published important theories.
subject + irregular past verbfinished actions with irregular verbsHe became world famous.
did not + base verbnegative pastHe did not stay in Germany.
Did + subject + base verb?past questionsDid he win the Nobel Prize?
