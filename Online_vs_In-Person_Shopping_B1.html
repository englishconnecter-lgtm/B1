Online vs In-Person Shopping

B1 video lesson on shopping habits, preferences and buying products online or in stores.

Open video on YouTube

Lesson Overview

Students watch a short street-interview video about what people like to buy online and what they prefer to buy in person. The lesson practises B1 language for shopping habits, preferences, local stores and returning items.

Before you watch

  1. What do you usually buy online?
  2. What do you prefer to buy in person?
  3. Have you ever sent something back after buying it online?

B1 Grammar: Prefer / Like / Would Rather

Use these forms to talk about shopping preferences.

FormExample
prefer to + verbI prefer to buy shoes in person.
like to + verbI like to buy bedding online.
would rather + verbI would rather buy clothes in a store.
because + reasonI buy shoes in person because I need to try them on.

Sentence Transformations

Use “prefer to”.
I like buying shoes in person more.
Use “would rather”.
I prefer buying clothes in a store.
Use “because”.
I buy clothes in person. I want to touch and feel them.

Discussion

Role Play: Online or In Person?

Situation: Two friends need to buy several products: shoes, a laptop, bedding, books and clothes. They must decide what to buy online and what to buy in person.

Student A: Online Shopper

You prefer online shopping because it is quick and convenient. Explain your choices.

Student B: Local Shopper

You prefer local stores because you can see, touch and try products. Explain your choices.
