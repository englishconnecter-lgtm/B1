Misunderstandings at Work

B1 video lesson on clarification, communication problems and staying on the same page.

Open video on YouTube

Lesson Overview

Students watch a short construction-site video about misunderstandings. The lesson practises B1 language for asking for clarification, checking information and avoiding communication problems at work.

Before you watch

  1. Why do misunderstandings happen at work?
  2. What happens when people cannot hear clearly?
  3. How can a team check that everyone understands the plan?

B1 Grammar: Checking and Clarifying

Use these phrases when you are not sure you heard or understood correctly.

FunctionUseful phrase
Ask someone to repeatCould you repeat that, please?
Check meaningDo you mean the wall or the hall?
Confirm informationSo, we need to redo the wall, right?
Say you are confusedSorry, I’m not sure I understand.

Role Play: On the Same Page

Situation: A construction team receives unclear instructions. One person must ask for clarification before work begins.

Student A: Site Manager

Give instructions about a wall, a pipe and a hallway. Speak quickly at first, then clarify.

Student B: Worker

Ask clarification questions and confirm the correct plan.
