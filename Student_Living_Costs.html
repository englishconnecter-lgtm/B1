B1 Grammar: How Much / How Many + Quantifiers

Use how much for uncountable nouns and money. Use how many for countable plural nouns. Use quantifiers to talk about amounts.

FormUseExample
How much + uncountable noun?ask about money or amountsHow much money do students spend?
How many + plural noun?ask about numberHow many supermarkets are there?
a lot of / many / sometalk about amountThere are many supermarkets.
a little / a bit / aroundapproximate amountA bike costs around €50.
