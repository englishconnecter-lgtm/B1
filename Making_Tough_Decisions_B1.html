B1 Grammar: Should / Shouldn’t for Decision Advice

Use should and shouldn’t to give advice about choices and decisions.

FormExample
You should + verbYou should list the pros and cons.
You shouldn’t + verbYou shouldn’t avoid the decision.
Should I + verb?Should I choose the first option?
Why don’t you + verb?Why don’t you practise with smaller decisions?

Discussion

  1. Why are some decisions difficult?
  2. Is it worse to make the wrong choice or not choose at all?
  3. Do you usually list pros and cons?
  4. What decision do many students find difficult?
  5. Who helps you when you need to make a decision?
  6. Can small decisions help you practise for big decisions?
  7. What is one decision you need to make soon?
