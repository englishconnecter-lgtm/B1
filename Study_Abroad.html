B1 Grammar: Used to / Started to / Managed to

Use these forms to talk about habits, changes and success after difficulty.

FormUseExample
used to + verbpast habitsI used to see my family every day.
started to + verba change beginsI started to feel more confident.
managed to + verbsucceeded after difficultyI managed to adjust to the new culture.

Role Play: Helping an International Student

Situation: A new international student feels homesick and overwhelmed. Another student gives advice.

Student A: New Student

Explain your problems: you miss home, you do not understand local phrases, transport is confusing and you feel lonely.

Student B: Student Mentor

Give practical advice: speak to welfare advisors, decorate your room, join societies, cook with flatmates and talk to people.
