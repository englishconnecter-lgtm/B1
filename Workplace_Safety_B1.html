B1 video lesson on workplace accidents, injuries, hazards and simple safety advice.

Lesson Overview

Students watch a workplace safety video about slips, trips and falls. The lesson practises B1 language for accidents, injuries, workplace hazards and simple safety advice.

Before you watch

  1. What things can make people fall at work?
  2. Which workplaces are more dangerous: offices, factories or construction sites?
  3. What safety rules do people sometimes ignore?

Comprehension

Click each question to reveal the answer.

0:04How many workplace accidents happen in Finland every year because of slips, trips and falls?
More than 20,000 accidents happen every year.
0:12How many days off work do these accidents cause?
They cause employees to take more than 350,000 days off work.
0:18What common injuries are mentioned?
People often strain, sprain or fracture their arms or legs.
0:34What happened in the first example?
An employee slipped when entering a building, fell down stairs and died from his injuries.
0:55Why did employees have to walk around a passageway?
Stuff had been left blocking the passageway.
1:20What happened at a construction site?
An employee tripped over cables and broke his wrist.
1:34How can workplaces prevent similar accidents?
They can plan workspaces and access routes so they are neat and tidy, and do risk assessments.
2:24What should employees do to stay safe?
They should take enough time, wear suitable shoes, focus on one thing, put things back and report dangers.

Role Play: Workplace Safety Check

Situation: A safety officer visits a workplace and notices several dangers: cables on the floor, poor lighting, blocked passageways and people working in a hurry.

Student A: Safety Officer

Explain the dangers and give clear safety instructions using imperatives.

Student B: Team Leader

Ask questions, explain why the problems happened and agree on actions to fix them.
