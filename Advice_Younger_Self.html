B1 Grammar: Giving Advice

We can give advice with imperatives, should, and don’t. The video uses many short advice phrases.

FormUseExample
Be + adjectivegive simple positive adviceBe fearless. / Be kind to yourself.
Don’t + verbgive negative adviceDon’t overthink the future.
You should + verbgive personal adviceYou should believe in yourself.
Try to + verbgive softer adviceTry to enjoy the present.

Role Play: Advice Interview

Situation: One student is a street interviewer. The other student answers questions about advice for their younger self.

Student A: Interviewer

Ask for one piece of advice. Ask follow-up questions: Why? Can you give an example? Do you follow this advice now?

Student B: Speaker

Give advice to your younger self. Explain your answer and react naturally.

Challenge

Use at least five phrases: be kind to yourself, don’t overthink, believe in yourself, take risks, save money, enjoy life.
