B1 Grammar: Imperatives for Travel Advice

Use imperatives to give clear travel tips and instructions.

FormUseExample
Verb + objectgive positive adviceBring a bottle and some snacks.
Don’t + verbgive negative adviceDon’t eat near sightseeing spots.
Make sure + clausestrong advice / reminderMake sure you don’t travel during school breaks.

Role Play: Plan a Budget Trip

Situation: Two friends want to travel to a European city with little money. They need to plan transport, accommodation, food and activities.

Student A: Budget Traveller

You want the cheapest possible trip. Suggest early flights, hostels, local food and walking.

Student B: Careful Traveller

You want to save money, but you also want comfort and safety. Ask questions and negotiate.
