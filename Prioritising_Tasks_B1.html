Lesson Overview

Students watch a short productivity video about prioritising tasks with the Eisenhower Matrix. The lesson practises B1 language for urgent and important tasks, deadlines, planning, delegating and avoiding time wasters.

Comprehension

Click each question to reveal the answer. Each question is synced to the video time.

0:00Who is the video for?
It is for people who have a lot to do, feel overwhelmed and do not know where to start.
0:16What are the two factors that define what we do?
The two factors are urgent and important.
0:19What does urgent mean?
Urgent means something requires attention right now and has immediate consequences.
0:26What does important mean?
Important means something helps us achieve long-term goals and values.
0:48What is the first step in using the Eisenhower Matrix?
The first step is to write a list of all the things you need to get done.
1:15What should you do with important and urgent tasks?
You should do them first and do them now.
1:25What should you do with important but not urgent tasks?
You should schedule them and decide when to do them.
1:42What should you do with urgent but not important tasks?
You should reschedule or delegate them.
1:59What should you do with tasks that are not urgent and not important?
You should do them later or avoid them completely.

B1 Grammar: Should / Need to / Imperatives for Advice

Use these forms to give advice about tasks and priorities.

FormExample
should + verbYou should do urgent tasks first.
need to + verbYou need to write a list.
imperativeSchedule important tasks.

Sentence Transformations

Use “should”.
Do urgent tasks first.
Use “need to”.
Write a list of tasks.
Use an imperative.
You should schedule important tasks.
